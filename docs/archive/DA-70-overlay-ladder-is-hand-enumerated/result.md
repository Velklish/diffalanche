**Closed 2026-09-19.** Completed. `src/ui/overlays.ts` is the ladder: one row per overlay, topmost first, each carrying the store flag that opens it and the action that closes it, with `topOverlay` and `closeTopOverlay` over the list. `keys.ts` asks it instead of holding two written-out sets of flags. `esc` closes the top one **and nothing else**, so a press an overlay answered no longer falls through to `closeComposer()` and the draft; a letter under any overlay belongs to the overlay, which closes the third symptom the card found — `c` under the open scope editor opened a composer on the file behind it. Adding an overlay is a row in that list and no edit to `keys.ts`. **The decision the card left open**, taken by the orchestrator on the bus: `⌘K` and `⇧⇧` over an overlay that is not the palette are **refused**, not a replacement — "one overlay at a time" is the rule the reference already states, and replacing would mean every overlay knowing how to close every other.
