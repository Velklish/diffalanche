**Closed 2026-09-24.** Completed. `src/core/ml/suggest` brings the index up to date, embeds the text and takes its five nearest comments across every session, each with its similarity, severity, session, file and line, and the severity they vote for with a confidence; `diffalanche suggest --body <text> [--json]` and `GET /api/suggest?body=` answer with it. Decisions this acceptance records:

- **k = 5, τ = 0.01, floor 0.86**, chosen with `perf/suggest-vote.ts` on 40 labelled comments — ten findings in four wordings, two English and two Russian — and eight unrelated texts: each neighbour weighs `exp((similarity − nearest) / τ)`, the confidence is the winner's share, and nothing is proposed under the floor. At k 5 and τ 0.01 all 40 severities came out right. The margins of the two Russian paraphrases the tests use, 0.9244 and 0.8823 on darwin-arm64, are recorded, and the assertion prints its similarity. - **The server answers from a worker thread** (DA-34.1) started by the first `/api/suggest` and holds the index it last read; an update that finds nothing changed hands back the index as it is. Warm `/api/suggest` took 5–7 ms over 200 comments and 21–36 ms over 10 000 (medians, loads 28–40). A missing model or a thread that cannot load is a 503 while the process lives; `ReviewServer.close` ends the thread, and a dead one is started again by the next request. A blank `--body` and a root nobody reviewed are refused in one line.
