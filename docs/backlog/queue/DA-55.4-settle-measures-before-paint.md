# DA-55.4 · The reading position can be lost when settle() measures a frame that has not painted yet

- **Order:** 340
- **Scope:** 08-ui (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-10
- **Dependencies:** none

## Context

`e2e/live.spec.ts` — *an edit patches its own card, holds the reading position, and leaves the composer open* — went red once in six runs of `bun run test:ui` during DA-55, at a load average of 14.8. The assertion is `expect(Math.abs(after.top - marks.top)).toBeLessThan(ROW_HEIGHT)`, and it received **44** against a bound of 22.

44 is exactly two diff rows of 22 px, and the edit the test makes adds exactly two lines. So the number does not point at a partial correction that fell short: it says **the correction did not run at all**. Any cause of a missed correction produces the same 44, which is why the figure on its own identifies nothing.

One cause was ruled out by measurement rather than argument. The same test failed with the same 44 earlier in the same run, during DA-54, and there the reason was real: the probe in `capture()` anchored on the sticky repository bar, which never moves, so there was nothing to correct against. That could plausibly have come back from the other side once DA-55 put a SCOPE pill in the header and a footer under the tree. It has not: probing `document.elementFromPoint` down from `PROBE_Y = 100` in four states — current session, select mode on, a scoped task, and a scoped task with select mode on — captures `.file-head` every time, `position: static`, with no `position: sticky` anywhere on the chain up to `.centre`. The header still ends at 52 px, the repository bar at 90 px, and the probe sits at 100 px in the 10 px of clearance DA-54 left it. `.composer-inner` is sticky on `left`, not `top`.

What remains is a hypothesis, and it is about the product rather than the test. `settle()` waits for `afterPaint()` and then compares `getBoundingClientRect`. If the frame carrying the new diff has not painted when the measurement is taken, the delta is zero, no scroll correction is issued, and the growth arrives afterwards — which is precisely "the reading position moved by the whole growth, and nothing corrected it". It would also explain why this only appears under load: a busy machine is where a frame is late.

Nobody has caught it in the act. Doing so means instrumenting `settle()` and waiting for a rare event, which is why it is written down here rather than chased at the end of a task.

## Work to do

- Instrument `settle()` — under a flag or in a harness rather than in shipped code — to record, per correction: whether `afterPaint` had actually delivered a painted frame, the delta it measured, and the growth that arrived afterwards. Run `e2e/live.spec.ts` under load until the red reproduces, and read the record.
- If the hypothesis holds, the fix is about what `settle()` waits for, not about the bound in the test. Raising 22 would hide it: the assertion is the only thing that notices the reader's place being lost.
- If it does not hold, the record will say what did happen, and this entry closes with that.

## Out of scope

- The probe's position and what it anchors on: measured and clean, in four states, above.
- Test flakiness in general. This entry is about one assertion whose failure means a reader lost their place on a real page, not about a test that is merely unreliable.

## Verification

- `bun run test:ui` is green across ten runs at a load average above 15, or the mechanism is named with a recorded instance and fixed.
- The record shows what `settle()` measured in the failing case: a painted frame or not, and what the delta was.
