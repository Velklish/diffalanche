# DA-82.4 · The jump and composer lines could hold a frame count against a ceiling

- **Scope:** 11-perf (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-29
- **Dependencies:** none
- **Parent:** DA-82.3
- **Cost:** major

## Context

Finding discovered while working on DA-82.3. The unpaced harness charges a frame
with something to draw what drawing it costs, a millisecond or two, where the
reader's 60 Hz display charges up to 16.7 ms, so a regression that only adds such
frames is outside the gate's `ms` lines; DA-82.3 wrote that exclusion into
[11-perf.md](../../reference/11-perf.md#what-a-frame-costs-unpaced-and-what-the-gate-does-not-see)
with its measurements.

Evidence, the 4-core cloud container of 2026-09-29, Chromium 141 headless shell,
one-minute load 0.9–2.3 on 4 cores: a `requestAnimationFrame` counter installed
from the harness side (`page.evaluate`, no change to `src/ui`) around each call
counted the frames the call spans, five `perf`-shaped processes a variant:

- the jump, `perf.jumpToFile`: 3 frames in 15 of 15 jumps on the page as it ships,
  and 4 in 27 of 27 with an `await afterPaint()` added before the first scroll of
  `revealCard`, unpaced; the same 3 and 4 in 15 of 15 each at 60 Hz;
- the composer, `perf.openComposer`: 1 frame in 14 of 14;
- the session switch: 6 to 62 frames, since the counter keeps frames coming while
  the page waits for the server — it counts the wait, not the work.

So a frame count is cheap and was deterministic for the jump and the composer, and
is not a measure at all for the switch, the update or the first render. A ceiling
on it is a new row of the budget table in `perf/budgets.ts`, and whether the gate
gets one is not a worker's call.

## Work to do

- Decide whether `fileJumpMs` and `composerOpenMs` carry the frames they span and
  a ceiling on them (3 and 1 today), or whether the exclusion in 11-perf stands.
- If they do: the counter in `perf/harness.ts`, a row in `perf/budgets.ts`, and the
  count checked across five repetitions on the development machine before it gates.

## Out of scope

- The frame-rate flag, which is DA-115's decision.
- A frame count for the switch, the update or the first render, whose windows are
  mostly a wait for the server.

## Verification

- An `await afterPaint()` added before the first scroll of `revealCard` turns the
  gate red, or the reason it does not stays in 11-perf.
