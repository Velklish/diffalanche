# DA-35.1 · The suggestion floor of 0.86 was drawn on forty written texts, and wants real history

- **Scope:** 09-ml (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-24
- **Parent:** DA-35
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-35, raised by its review. `suggest` proposes
no severity when the nearest comment is less similar than 0.86. The number was
chosen by `perf/suggest-vote.ts` on forty comments written for the purpose — ten
findings in four wordings, two English and two Russian — and eight unrelated
texts: the forty's nearest similarities were 0.866–0.945, the eight's
0.802–0.859, a gap of 0.007 with no held-out set
([09-ml.md](../../reference/09-ml.md#suggestions)).

The cost is already visible on the small synthetic review: "Ключ кэша не
учитывает регион" finds the four region comments first at 0.848 and proposes
nothing, while its neighbours are right. The two Russian paraphrases the tests
use sit at 0.9244 and 0.8823 on darwin-arm64; another platform's kernels move a
vector by up to 0.0013 (ADR-014), so they are not at risk today, but a shorter
text in the other language is.

## Work to do

- Measure the nearest similarities of real comments against their own history —
  a leave-one-out over a data directory with a few hundred comments by more than
  one reviewer, in both languages — and set the floor, `k` and the temperature
  from that, with the method `perf/suggest-vote.ts` already has.
- Return condition: a data directory with at least 300 real comments exists to
  measure on (DA-36's composer in use makes one).
