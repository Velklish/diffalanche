# DA-96.2 · At the top of the page the sidebar footer sits under the status bar

- **Scope:** 08-ui (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-24
- **Parent:** DA-96.1
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-96.1, whose `disconnected` and `reconnect` live in the
sidebar footer. Measured on the base `660190b` (its UI built in a detached worktree), Chromium
through Playwright, a 1560 × 900 window, the small synthetic review, with
`getBoundingClientRect()`:

```
scroll 0:    .sidebar-foot 881–909 px, the status bar 870–900 px, .sidebar 91–909 px
scroll 400:  .sidebar-foot 842–870 px
```

The sidebar sticks under the header only once the page has scrolled about 40 px; before that
its bottom row runs 9 px past the window, and the fixed status bar covers the footer's line
whole. A reader who has not scrolled — which is where a page opens, and where a `reload` puts
it — does not see `watching`, `reconnecting` or `disconnected`, nor `reconnect`, until they
scroll. The e2e specs read the footer by its text, which does not need it to be visible, so
none of them notices. Not reproduced at other window heights.
