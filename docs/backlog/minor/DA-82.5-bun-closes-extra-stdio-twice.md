# DA-82.5 · Bun closes a closed child's extra stdio pipes a second time; not reported upstream

- **Scope:** 11-perf (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-29
- **Parent:** DA-82.2
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-82.2, on the 4-core cloud container of
2026-09-29 (Bun 1.3.14, Chromium 141 headless shell). The one-process loop of
`perf/run.ts --runs 3` as it was before DA-82.2 stalled in each of six attempts;
under `strace -f -e trace=close,pipe,pipe2,dup2,dup3,socketpair,execve` the
harness's main thread made four `socketpair` calls for each browser, giving the
child descriptors 3 and 4 from the parent's 20 and 22 both times, closed 20 and
22 when the first browser closed (01:03:55.341), and closed 20 and 22 again at
01:04:06.514, eleven seconds into the second browser, which then logged
`Connection terminated while reading from pipe` and exited with code 0. Nothing
in the harness closes a descriptor by number. The trace is not kept; the steps
to take it again are in
[11-perf.md](../../reference/11-perf.md#the-gate), "Why a second browser in one
process stalls".

It is Bun's, not the harness's; that it is triggered by a child spawned with
more than three stdio entries and not by children in general is an assumption,
from which descriptors were closed twice. A forced `Bun.gc(true)` after each
measurement let two of three runs finish, so a collection is involved in some
way that is not pinned down.

Worth doing when someone has the time: a reduced repro — spawn a child with
`stdio: ["pipe", "pipe", "pipe", "pipe", "pipe"]`, let it exit, spawn a second,
watch its descriptors 3 and 4 — reported to Bun, and the one-process loop tried
again on a Bun that fixes it. The harness runs one browser a process either
way, so nothing waits on it.
