## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-56.6.

**This is DA-56.6's own Verification, moved here.** DA-56.6 closes on its cause and its fix; the reading its card asked for cannot be taken on the machine it was worked on, so it lives on in this entry rather than being dropped.

DA-56.6's verification asks for `bun run perf` on a quiet machine measuring `Update after an edit in one repository` inside 300 ms three times on the tip of the main branch. Its fix was measured on a 4-core cloud container only, where the untouched base is over budget on other lines and a budget verdict is not evidence ([11-perf.md](../../reference/11-perf.md), "A red the machine caused"). What the container did show is relative: nine a side with `perf/compare.ts`'s loop, `1079222` was +81 ms over `4be4936` at the median, and the one condition DA-56.6 adds took back 77 ms of it on `1079222` (the full table is in 11-perf.md under "What inside DA-55 carries it"). At the tip the container gave less back: `bun perf/compare.ts` called the fix better by 52 and 43 ms in two runs of three (−25 in the third, no difference), against a step of 57 ms between `4be4936` and the unfixed tip — 25–52 of 57. What is left above `4be4936` is DA-56.11's. Whether the M1 Pro now reads under 300 ms is an assumption from those proportions, not a reading: its tip was at 347, 352 and 392 ms before the fix.
