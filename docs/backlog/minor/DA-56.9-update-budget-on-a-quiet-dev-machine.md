- **Scope:** 11-perf (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-29
- **Parent:** DA-56.6

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-56.6.

DA-56.6's verification asks for `bun run perf` on a quiet machine measuring `Update after an edit in one repository` inside 300 ms three times on the tip of the main branch. Its fix was measured on a 4-core cloud container only, where the untouched base is over budget on other lines and a budget verdict is not evidence ([11-perf.md](../../reference/11-perf.md), "A red the machine caused"). What the container did show is relative: nine a side with `perf/compare.ts`'s loop, `1079222` was +81 ms over `4be4936` at the median, and the one condition DA-56.6 adds took back 77 ms of it on `1079222` (the full table is in 11-perf.md under "What inside DA-55 carries it"). Whether the M1 Pro now reads under 300 ms is an assumption from that proportion, not a reading: its tip was at 347–392 ms before the fix, and 77 ms off 347 is 270.
