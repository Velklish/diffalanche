# DA-85.1 · A runtime that never had a recursive watch starts on the walk and says nothing

- **Order:** 255
- **Scope:** 05-watcher (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-09-18
- **Dependencies:** none

## Context

DA-85 gave the mid-session takeover a signal: a watch that dies after it started
hands its tree to the walk, and `onFallback` says so once, which `serve` prints.
The other way into the walk is still silent.

Two paths reach it, neither of them a failure the handler sees:

- the probe answers `false` — `recursive = options.recursive ?? await supportsRecursiveWatch(config.dataDir)`
  at [src/core/watcher/index.ts:114](../../../src/core/watcher/index.ts), so every
  `watchTree` is then called with `recursive: false` and takes the walk at
  construction;
- `watch()` itself throws — `native` returns `null`
  ([src/core/watcher/tree.ts:104-128](../../../src/core/watcher/tree.ts)), which is
  what Node's `ERR_FEATURE_UNAVAILABLE_ON_PLATFORM` does, and
  `current ??= polling(options)` takes over without going through `fallBack`.

In both the whole session runs on a 250 ms walk and cannot meet the budget of
`docs/SPEC.md` section 6 — the same consequence DA-85 judged worth a line — while
the operator is told nothing. `docs/reference/05-watcher.md` now says this
explicitly rather than implying `onFallback` covers it.

Why it was not folded into DA-85: that entry is scoped to "what happens after the
watch is gone", and the two cases differ in what the answer should be. A takeover
mid-session has a blind window and the tree is re-read whole to close it; here the
walk's first baseline *is* the startup baseline, nothing was missed, and a rescan
of every repository right after the server's own scan would be waste. Only the
operator's line is wanted, which is a different change from the one DA-85 made.

Not verified: whether `recursive: false` passed deliberately — by `--no-recursive`
on `serve`, for a network mount — should be silent. It is the operator's own
choice, so a line about it may be noise; that is the decision this entry has to
take rather than a fact it can state.

## Work to do

- Decide what is said, and when. The candidates: a second callback that means
  "this session walks and always did"; the same `onFallback` fired at
  construction, with the caller told which of the two it is, so it does not
  rescan for it; or a line printed by `serve` itself from the answer of
  `supportsRecursiveWatch`, which is the one place that already knows before any
  tree is built.
- Decide whether an explicit `recursive: false` is included or exempt.
- Whatever is chosen, do not make the startup path re-read every repository:
  nothing was missed at startup, and DA-85's rescan-per-tree exists for a window
  that does not exist here.

## Out of scope

- The mid-session takeover, its window and its signal: that is DA-85, closed.
- Making a runtime recurse, and the probe itself (DA-92 covers its uncaught
  write).

## Verification

- A watcher built with `recursive: false` — or with a `native` that returns
  `null` — produces whatever signal is chosen, and a test names it.
- `serve` prints at most one line about it for a whole session, the way the
  takeover line does.
