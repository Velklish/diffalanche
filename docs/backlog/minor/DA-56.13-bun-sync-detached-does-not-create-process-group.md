# DA-56.13 · bun-sync-detached-does-not-create-process-group

- **Scope:** 11-perf (see [reference](../../reference/README.md))
- **Created:** 2026-11-01
- **Parent:** DA-56.10
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-56.10.

Evidence: Controlled Bun 1.3.14 macOS arm64 execFileSync with detached:true and a 300ms timeout returned ETIMEDOUT, pid11762, SIGKILL. Before timeout Python os.getpgid recorded parent11762 and child11763 in PGID11751, so killing -11762 found no group and the child remained observable after 500ms. Raw timeout-bun-group-python.json/.stderr in the harness-controls worker artifacts. The direct-child-only timeout bounds the controlled finite inherited-stdout fixture; full descendant cleanup is unproved and outside this fix. Future verification: reproduce an isolated synchronous detached launch and compare actual parent/child PGIDs and liveness under Node and Bun, without targeting the runner group.
