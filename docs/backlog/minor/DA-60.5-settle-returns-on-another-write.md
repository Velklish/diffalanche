# DA-60.5 · settle in tests/watcher.test.ts returns on any diff-changed of the other repository, not on its own write's

- **Scope:** 11-perf
- **Created:** 2026-09-24
- **Parent:** DA-60.2
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-60.2. `settle` writes a file of its own
into the other repository and returns on the first `diff-changed` of that
repository after its mark, whichever write it is about:

<!-- quote:../../../tests/watcher.test.ts -->
```ts
    if (changesOf(mark, OTHER_REPO).length > 0) return;
```
<!-- /quote -->

A rescan waits at least the 100 ms of the debounce after the write, yet in DA-60.2's
18 loaded runs of the whole suite on Bun (the probe logged every settle's time from
its write to its return) the settles after the late one returned in 5, 7, 11, 11,
13, 17, 35, 42, 43, 46, 50, 53, 57 and 72 ms — on the event of an earlier write,
the late settle's own among them. A settle that returns on somebody else's event
proves nothing about the queue behind its own write, and a test that looks for an
absence right after it may look too early: a green that means nothing, which is the
kind of wait 11-perf's "Waits in the suites" is against.

## What it would take

Match the event to the write: return on a `diff-changed` whose `files` name the
settle's own file, as the overdue report DA-60.2 added already does. Check first
that no test leans on the loose match.
