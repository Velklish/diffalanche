# DA-34.3 · The model's own process peaks 17 MiB under ADR-014's ceiling on Bun

- **Scope:** 09-ml
- **Created:** 2026-09-24
- **Parent:** DA-34.2
- **Cost:** minor

## Evidence

Finding discovered while working on DA-34.2. Since DA-34.2 the model runs in a
process of its own, which is exactly the process ADR-014's ceiling describes:

<!-- quote:../../adr/adr-014-embedding-model-and-npm-delivery.md -->
- **Memory ceiling: 768 MiB** peak resident size for a process that loads the
  model and embeds 200 comments. The highest of the 16 runs of this pair was 651
  MiB, and the ceiling is 18 % above it.
<!-- /quote -->

Measured on 2026-09-24 on the development machine (Apple M1 Pro, 8 cores, 16 GB,
load averages 6.8–10.2), each process's own `maxRSS`, with
`/usr/bin/time -l <runtime> perf/index-scale.ts query --data-dir <d>` and
`… serve --root <fixture> [--keep-index]`, two runs a cell: the model's process
peaked at 627–737 MiB over every cell — 660–737 MiB on Bun 1.3.14, 627–706 MiB
on Node 25.2.1 — whatever the index held
([09-ml.md](../../reference/09-ml.md#in-a-process-of-its-own)). In ADR-014's own
shape — the model loaded and 200 texts embedded, driven through the protocol,
three runs of each way to start it — it peaked at 710–751 MiB on Bun (the
binary 710–742, `child-entry.ts` 710–747, and an earlier series of the two 735–739
and 729–751) and 681–707 MiB on Node. The highest, 751 MiB, is 17 MiB under the
ceiling.

**For the owner — the lever is the owner's.** The model's process on Bun peaks
at up to 751 MiB, 17 MiB under the ceiling, and that is the model's own weight:
nothing the index or the process split does lowers it. The levers are ADR-014's — another model, another
runtime, or the ceiling restated for the model's process. Not measured, and so an
assumption: the model alone measured 476–650 MiB in ADR-014's runs and 697–751
MiB on 2026-09-23 and 24, so the machine's memory pressure moves it by tens of
MiB, and 17 MiB of headroom may not hold on another machine.

## Owner's decision (2026-09-24)

Keep the model and ADR-014's ceiling, and watch the headroom: the entry stays open as a watch item. How it is watched is the orchestrator's reading, not the owner's words: the model's process is measured again when the model, the runtime or `onnxruntime-node` changes, and the numbers go back to the owner.
